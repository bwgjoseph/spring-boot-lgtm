# Alertmanager: Alert Routing & Deduplication Requirements

## 1. Role & Responsibility (The "Job")
Alertmanager is the **Notification Dispatcher** for the production cluster. Its "job" is to handle alerts generated by Prometheus/Mimir, perform deduplication, grouping, and silencing, and dispatch them to notification channels.

## 2. Notification Reliability
- **Channel:** All critical alerts must be delivered to the **Mattermost** central notification channel.
- **Deduplication:** Alerts must be grouped by `['alertname', 'cluster', 'service', 'namespace']` to consolidate related issues into single notifications.

## 3. Alert Routing
- **Grouping:** Use `group_wait: 30s` and `group_interval: 5m` for efficient notification delivery.
- **Resolution:** `send_resolved: true` is mandatory to ensure visibility when a failure is fixed.
